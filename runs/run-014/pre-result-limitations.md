# Interpretation checks identified before empirical scores

These are limitations and possible follow-up questions, not completed experiments or claims of what the results show. Times and fixed plans are indexed in events.jsonl.

* All cases supply an initial cover class; the task can be solved by classifying subsequent cover. The v6/v7 comparisons address this explicitly. A result is not fully label-free change detection.
* Reference/control and supervised readouts use different subsets of the available earlier labels. Offering the same pool is not an annotation-budget-matched experiment. No user benefit, field-visit saving or annotation-cost saving is measured. A future comparison could fit a state readout using only initial-state labels or the labelled baseline cohorts, but it is not yet an executed analysis.
* Global baseline tree-cover plots are not verified reference ecosystems. Their later stability is not known; real within-class change and representation/observation variability cannot be separated causally. Nearest-available plots can remain geographically distant, especially after region holdout. Baseline non-tree control matching is a boundary check, not proof of environmental matching.
* The original benchmark is change-enriched, contains one selected adjacent pair per plot and has a highly uneven calendar distribution of tree gains. Its source methods do not establish a national transition-frequency sample. Gain recall is based on few later positive cases. The extension has a different Forest definition and few gains beyond2021 as well; do not pool the endpoints or attribute their difference to time alone.
* The encoder products and manual labels were produced later than the represented years. The holdout is downstream and retrospective, not historical deployment or encoder-pretraining isolation.
* Spatial resampling intervals condition on fitted scores, a fixed reference pool and only two original later year-pairs. At observed zero-error floors/ceilings, the resampling distribution can be degenerate; that does not demonstrate zero population error. Counts, block counts, reference-seed sensitivity and explicit scope matter more than a narrow-looking interval.
* A constant control subtraction preserves within-year/region rankings by algebra. Benefits can only be judged from transferred flags; neither mean centring nor a smoother series validates a proxy. Pooled rankings can differ when offsets differ across groups.
* AlphaEarth is sampled from an unofficial geographic mosaic. The native-grid check is a stratified co-location diagnostic, not full native-grid classification evaluation. High vector agreement would not rule out small threshold effects. Centre/patch sensitivity is available, but exact30m label footprints and ecological attributes remain unmeasured.
* Trees/Forest transitions are only specified cover endpoints. Success cannot establish ecological restoration success; failure cannot rule out sensitivity to useful unmeasured within-class change. LCMAP Trees includes standing dead trees. The NLCD height/cover threshold and its young-tree Shrubland definition differ.
* The added standard and supervised comparisons are fixed before scores. No winner is chosen on the test set. If further metrics, tuning, exclusions or hypotheses are chosen after scores, mark them exploratory and fix a new plan before running them.
