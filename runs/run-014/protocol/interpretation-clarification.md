# Pre-analysis clarification of temporal scope

The temporal holdout in all plans is at the downstream readout/calibration level. “Earlier” means the calendar year assigned to the label or embedding, not that the entire published instrument and manually interpreted label set were operationally available then. The encoders, reference interpretations and their public releases were produced later. TimeSync interpretation can use a full image time series; AlphaEarth training includes historical land-cover targets. This experiment is a retrospective test on earlier versus later represented years, not a reconstruction of information available to an operator in2019, an encoder-pretraining temporal holdout, or a prospective field trial.

The baseline-only reference rule prohibits selecting controls on later target outcomes, but does not erase the retrospective production of baseline labels. No new outcome-dependent selection is introduced. These limits follow from the checked original methods and metadata, before empirical score inspection, and must be stated in the paper. They do not change any numerical method or exclusion rule. A change in a score cannot by itself identify observation/representation drift separately from real within-class environmental change.
