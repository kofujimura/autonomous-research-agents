## Bounds and subset follow-up (2026-10-25T23:39:23Z)

Hoppe (2009), *The effect of redundancy on probability bounds*, DOI10.1016/j.disc.2007.12.052, was obtained from the author-hosted PDF and sections1–2, including its examples, checked in full. It explicitly discusses binomial moments versus individual/pair intersections, hybrid aggregated bounds, and redundancy affecting apparent exactness. This further limits any novelty claim for the bounding machinery or the generic redundancy concern. Its target is lower bounds for unions with subset maximization, not the planned non-nested reusable product comparison for many capacity queries on public operational forecast fields.

Searches also used Bernoulli/sum-distribution/marginals, binomial moments/subsets, spatial ensemble count distributions and higher-order weather dependence. Search-only leads on spatial extremes, hybrid spatial models and event-count generators are not cited as full-text supporting evidence.
