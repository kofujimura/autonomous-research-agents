# Pre-execution plan v1

This plan follows the completed topic-selection decision (commit 51640d905080f637c8f9672f568321b0bf6af80e). Its fixing time and commit are recorded in events.jsonl before the first main acquisition/measurement. No main label arrays, trained classifiers, diversity changes or audit outcomes have been examined. The US feasibility pilot is excluded from the main study. The public Swiss class metadata contains national annual areas; these were visible during taxonomy inspection but are not the landscape-change outcomes tested here.

## Question and intended use

At a fixed number of new reference labels per year, how reliably can model-assisted estimates using frozen classifiers of annual satellite embeddings identify changes in landscape crop-group diversity? Does revisiting the same reference locations improve inference compared with independent annual samples?

The intended decision is the maintenance of reference samples for retrospective agricultural monitoring. The study does not measure field-visit costs, adoption, farm decisions, ecosystem outcomes, or a policy effect. Sampling/quantification methods are established; the empirical contribution, if useful, is their behavior for this change estimand under actual crop turnover and annual representation shifts.

## Tests fixed before results

1. Compare paired and independent annual reference samples using direct class proportions and model-assisted proportions. Test whether the variance advantage of repeated locations persists after model assistance. The null/negative possibility is retained: pairing may help little or hurt when residual annual contributions are negatively correlated.
2. Compare the error, direction-reporting frequency, and wrong-direction reporting of reference-based methods at fixed budgets against unadjusted model counts and a no-change baseline. No improvement is assumed. A method that rarely reports any direction is not operationally successful merely because it seldom reports a wrong one.
3. Check how conclusions vary across two independently released annual representations, published taxonomy levels, and three adjacent target-year transitions. This is a bounded empirical comparison, not a claim about every representation, country, or agricultural system.

Primary outcomes: Monte Carlo mean squared error (MSE) of change in Shannon entropy, paired/independent MSE ratio within method; empirical coverage of nominal 95% intervals; fraction of intervals excluding zero; and unconditional fraction reporting the wrong direction. Conditional wrong direction among reports is also shown, with its denominator. Report all eligible units, including small/zero changes, absent classes and entropy boundaries. No significance-screened subset is primary.

## Population, geography, split and stopping rules

Use SwissCrop25's UTM32N grid. Define disjoint 4×4 groups of its 1.28 km tiles: 5.12×5.12 km landscapes, with one tile-width gap between candidate groups in both directions. Resolve exact tile anchoring from the public coordinate metadata; document the deterministic formula. Require label-tile coverage in all five years. Do not select places by the magnitude or sign of change.

Order candidate landscapes with a seeded random permutation (seed 16016). Aim for 60 landscapes: 20 train, 10 source-calibration, 30 test. Assign spatial groups before looking at changes: use a deterministic hash of 25.6 km supercells to divide candidate groups into train/calibration/test pools in proportions 1/3, 1/6, 1/2. All years of a location stay in its split. Require at least 10% of landscape pixels to have valid agricultural labels throughout 2021–2025 and at least 4,096 such pixels. In each split take the first eligible landscapes until its quota; inspect at most 200 candidate landscapes total. If fewer are available or resources/time prevent the quotas, report the attained number and reason; do not change eligibility in pursuit of an effect. Save the full attempted-candidate list, reasons and order, including failures. This is a convenience benchmark from licensed complete data, not a probability sample representative of Switzerland.

Primary support is agricultural pixels with reference labels in all five years. This fixed support isolates crop-composition change from changes in declared extent; it is a restricted estimand. Record 2021 agricultural area, retained area, missing/non-crop transitions, and exclusions. Select N=4,096 distinct equal-area 10 m pixel centers uniformly without replacement on that support per landscape (seed derived from 16016 and landscape ID). These form a saved finite population. Estimate no household/farm values. Save exact whole-support class counts as a sampling-resolution diagnostic, but primary audit experiments target the saved finite population, whose true proportions are known exactly. A secondary comparison quantifies the difference from whole-support entropy changes.

Use common valid locations for both representations and all five years. Record missingness; if this leaves fewer than 2,048 finite-population points in a landscape, exclude it with the reason and retain its raw attempts. Do not replace failed data based on classification performance or diversity change. Quotas may therefore be missed. The reference label/value and exact sampled coordinate transforms must be checked against raster metadata; do not treat resampled labels as independent pixels.

Primary taxonomy is SwissCrop25's existing Crop_Label_lv2 agricultural groups, including grassland/permanent agriculture as defined there; refer to the outcome as crop-group diversity. Exclude the five non-crop groups and undefined labels. Freeze the mapping using the published CSV and label-store attributes. Secondary taxonomy: Crop_Label_lv1, with the same support; rare/unseen classes remain in the target rather than being removed after results. No new class aggregation selected by effect size. Decode original LNF codes or stored class names explicitly, saving the lookup and any ambiguities.

## Representations and prediction comparisons

Sample the nearest native 10 m embedding pixel at each reference point using documented CRS transforms. Disable nearby-valid searches. Record pixel displacement (should be at most half the pixel diagonal in native CRS) and check north/south orientation against known metadata/array indices. Do not interpret a different reference-grid origin as additional independent labels. Save numerical features and labels, enough to recompute offline without downloading again.

Fit, separately for each representation and taxonomy, an unweighted multinomial L2 logistic regression (C=1, intercept, max_iter=1,000, tolerance=1e-5) after training-set standardization. Training uses only 2021 and the train landscapes; use at most the saved 4,096 points per landscape. No hyperparameter search or outcome-driven model selection. Save convergence diagnostics. A fixed random-forest comparison (200 trees, min_samples_leaf=5, sqrt features, seed 16016, at most 4 threads) is preplanned as a robustness check if the primary fit converges and resources permit. Preserve a failed fit and fix only implementation errors before a new recorded run.

Freeze each fitted classifier for 2022–2025. Source-calibration landscapes in 2021 supply class-conditional prediction matrices for source-confusion adjustment; they do not train the classifier. Test landscapes never train or calibrate it. Save per-point probabilities in a fixed full taxonomy, assigning zero probability to classes absent from training and counting those classes and their target mass. A separately trained current-year logistic regression on train landscapes is a diagnostic comparison using many new labels; its extra label burden is explicit and it is not budget-matched to a small audit.

Zero-new-label comparisons: hard predicted class proportions; soft probability averages; constrained least-squares source-confusion quantification, minimizing ||C p - q||² over the simplex using 2021 calibration and target soft means; and no change. Record matrix rank/condition number and source support. The constrained quantifier is a regularized implementation of an established label-shift approach, not guaranteed valid under phenological/covariate shift.

## Reference sampling and estimators

Primary transitions: 2022→2023, 2023→2024, 2024→2025, all after the frozen training year. The 2021→2022 transition is secondary. Each transition/landscape has a known finite-population reference change ΔH=H(p_b)-H(p_a), H(p)=-Σ p_c ln p_c and 0 ln 0=0. Use natural logarithms. exp(H) is the effective number of the specified groups; its ratio is exp(ΔH), not a species count.

Annual budgets n={32,64,128,256,512}; each two-year contrast costs 2n reference labels. Revisited design draws n locations by simple random sampling without replacement, uses those same locations in both years. Independent design draws n separately in each year. Sampling seeds derive reproducibly from seed 16016, landscape, transition and replication. Use 500 Monte Carlo replications per unit, method and budget, with common random draws across representations/methods. Report actual reference-label counts; do not claim equal travel/annotation costs across designs. These are resampling experiments on public reference data, not observations of actual auditors.

Direct estimator: sample class proportions. Model-assisted difference estimator for each year: mean of full-population predicted probabilities plus the sample mean of (one-hot reference label minus prediction). The latter is design-unbiased for proportions before any projection. If outside the probability simplex, use Euclidean simplex projection, log the frequency/magnitude, and acknowledge resulting finite-sample bias. Entropy is nonlinear and the estimated change need not be unbiased. The same code applies to direct estimation by setting predictions to zero and using the sample proportions.

Nominal 95% intervals use a delta-method linearization at the estimated class proportions. For numerical evaluation, clip gradient arguments at 10^-6; retain zero proportions in entropy. Influence values are -(ln p_c+1) dotted with per-point residual vectors. For paired samples use the sample variance of the difference of the two year-specific influences; for independent samples add the two sample variances. Apply finite-population correction (1-n/N), divide by n, and multiply standard error by 1.96. These are approximate intervals with empirical coverage tested here, not a distribution-free certification guarantee. A direction is reported only when the entire interval is above or below zero. True zero changes have no correct directional report; count them separately.

To separate the sampling-design comparison from plug-in gradient/boundary failures, also compute exact finite-population influence variances using reference p and fixed predictions. Report residual covariance and the predicted paired/independent variance ratio. This oracle diagnostic is not available operationally and is never substituted for a reporting interval. Normal-approximation minimum detectable change for 80% power is (1.96+0.8416212336) times the design standard error; label it an approximation, alongside actual reporting rates. Do not call a mathematically implied covariance identity a new empirical finding.

## Analysis and preplanned robustness

Primary summaries average equally over test landscapes and transitions, with separate results by representation and budget. Also show medians/distributions and paired contrasts across landscapes; temporal contrasts from one landscape are not independent landscape replicates. Monte Carlo standard errors accompany coverage/reporting rates. No unplanned p-value testing or broad population confidence claim. Record exact number of units with |ΔH|<0.01, H<0.05, a single observed group, unseen target mass, or identical compared predictions. In the abstract and main tables, state these counts if they affect interpretation.

Preplanned checks: (a) published finer hierarchy; (b) random forest instead of logistic; (c) same-year refreshed logistic diagnostic; (d) 2021→2022 and longer 2022→2025 changes; (e) whole-support versus finite-population reference; (f) sensitivity of sign to reference errors by contaminating 1%, 2%, and 5% of label mass, including a conservative entropy-continuity bound rather than asserting those rates are measured; (g) separating arable-dominated and grassland-dominated landscapes using 2021 declarations only. These checks retain the main question. New checks selected after results require a versioned plan, an event/time/reason and exploratory labeling.

Before interpreting unexpectedly poor interval coverage, check simplex projection, zero/small class proportions and model support. If feasible, add a prospectively versioned bootstrap or conservative alternative interval rather than recommending an unreliable method. Do not conceal the original interval's failures. The study is worthwhile even if it concludes that no small budget supports reliable direction reporting.
