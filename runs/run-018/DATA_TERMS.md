# Input provenance and reuse

The research uses public ecological inventories, numerical environmental rasters and published satellite embeddings. Retrieval dates, original URLs, licences/use statements, source snapshots and hashes are recorded in `sources.jsonl` and per-region acquisition records. The study does not link these data to people, households, businesses or ownership records. No external publication or upload was performed.

- **NWI/FWS:** the saved official global metadata reports no access/use restrictions. Credit the US Fish and Wildlife Service/National Wetlands Inventory. Source-image metadata dates describe mapping inputs, not measured annual hydroperiod.
- **AlphaEarth Foundations:** CC BY 4.0. Required credit: “The AlphaEarth Foundations Satellite Embedding dataset is produced by Google and Google DeepMind.” The exact mirror checkpoint is not independently confirmed; the archived official catalogue and mirror metadata are distinguished.
- **TESSERA:** public statements encountered differ between CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA. All permit this research use. Preserve the original statements and attribution; retain share-alike treatment conservatively for redistributed TESSERA-derived arrays pending clarification of the applicable variant. This record does not resolve the upstream discrepancy or grant rights beyond the original terms. The research uses v1.1-dclimate and archives its exact metadata and sampled values.
- **JRC Global Surface Water:** Copernicus free reuse with acknowledgement and the dataset citation. Credit: Source: EC JRC/Google. Use the archived corrected v1.5/2024 distribution and its release notes; do not silently substitute a different release.
- **USGS 3DEP:** public-domain USGS data; acknowledge USGS. The numerical elevation service snapshot and varying underlying vintages are recorded.
- **ASF HAND:** public CC0 statement saved with provenance for the v1/2021 Copernicus GLO-30-derived HAND product. Credit ASF and the underlying Copernicus DEM source. This is a terrain-derived input, not a field water-level record.
- **Census frame:** the first downloaded cartographic KML advises against geographic analysis and remains **unused**. Its ZIP/metadata are retained as a feasibility record. The alternative 2024 TIGER/Line® State and Equivalent Entities product permits reuse with Census acknowledgement and is used only as a statistical sampling frame, with no ownership, jurisdiction or legal-boundary claims. TIGER/Line® is a registered trademark of the US Census Bureau. The exploratory centre selection does not by itself establish that a wetland experiment in those windows was executed.
- **Literature:** downloaded articles retain their original copyright and licence statements; they are reading/evidence sources, separate from the experimental datasets. Relevant passage checks and incomplete/abstract-only inspections are distinguished in the source log.

All measurements required for offline result regeneration are intended to be bundled. The large AlphaEarth locator index is retained locally but excluded from Git because it is acquisition metadata rather than a model-analysis input; its hash, origin and optional-copy procedure are documented in the verification instructions. Source arrays, failed measurements and raw model decisions are not excluded from the final hash inventory merely because they were unsuccessful.
