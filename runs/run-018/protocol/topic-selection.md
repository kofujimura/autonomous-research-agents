### Pre-mortem before adoption

**A possible successful claim:** In geographically separated, vegetation-matched wetland reference pools, public annual satellite representations add (or fail to add) water-regime matching information beyond explicit terrain and surface-water covariates when the screening budget is held fixed.

**Usefulness:** This would change what evidence restoration researchers and inventory practitioners require before using latent-space neighbours as preliminary references. A positive increment supports testing an augmented screening workflow; a small or negative increment argues for spending effort on explicit hydrological evidence rather than assuming generic semantic similarity is sufficient. A diagnostic of inventory-class agreement alone does not establish restoration benefit. Magnitude and boundary conditions, rather than a generic mismatch count, determine usefulness.

**Why now / bounded novelty:** Global annual embeddings became available recently, connecting representation learning to reference-based ecological assessment. Wetland hydrology and reference comparability are longstanding subjects. The searched sources did not yield this specific incremental, coverage-controlled comparison using both representations and withheld water-regime labels; absence within that search is not evidence that no such study exists.

**Three likely first objections and planned responses:**
1. Water-regime labels are noisy historical image interpretations. Use the published category itself as the endpoint, obtain imagery dates, separate recent/old/unknown coverage, inspect official definitions and a deterministic sample of label/geometry records. Do not call agreement current hydroperiod or ecological function. Field validation is unavailable, so the core claim is narrowed to inventory agreement.
2. A weak baseline or trivial candidate pools can manufacture an apparent gain. Compare geography, explicit hydrological distances, and a learned hydrological-only model to the identical learner augmented with each representation; fix coverage, count missing-class and single-class pools, and use spatial rather than random polygon splits. Include label-aware oracle and analytic random expectations only as diagnostic bounds.
3. Similarity may fail even though embeddings contain useful information, or spatial/vegetation correlations may explain performance. Control vegetation subclass and geographic pool, contrast raw similarity with supervised compatibility and decoders, and test spatial buffers, held-out settings, alternate years and context. Restrict conclusions to the sampled settings and products.

**Metric/proxy check:** Exact NWI water-regime-category agreement is the measured property in the claim. Its relation to actual current hydroperiod or functional equivalence is not validated. A reproducible sample will audit parser output, permitted class combinations, geometry interior coverage and imagery dates against the original records and official definitions. This checks the path to the category endpoint, not the category-to-field-hydrology gap; the latter remains an explicit unmeasured limitation.
