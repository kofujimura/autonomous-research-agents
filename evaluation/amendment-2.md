## 3. Basis

- **The human's words:** 「E2をIEICEの初版に含めたいと思います」 (2026-11-03).
- **Context from the same conversation:**
  - In arXiv v1, every rating of the five papers is by an AI model, and the paper lists this as a limitation.
  - Before this decision, the management agent advised two things. AI-only rating is the main weakness for a journal submission. E2 alone (five papers, three raters) would be thin as a separate paper.

## 4. Rules fixed by this amendment, before any E2 rating exists

- Every E2 rating received by the deadline in the request e-mail is reported in the first submission, whatever it says. No rating and no rater is excluded after a rating has been seen.
- Ratings received after that deadline are not used in the first submission. They are reported in the revision or a later version, with the date they arrived.
- If fewer raters than planned respond by the deadline, the paper reports the number received and the number asked.
- The author's ratings are reported separately from the colleagues' ratings (`preregistration.md` §6).
- How the ratings are summarised is written in `../e2-eval/README.md` before the first rating file is opened.
- The paper says that E2 was first planned as a separate paper and was moved into this paper by this amendment, with its date.
